## Candidates in neighboring districts

### AC Transit, Ward 3: **ADRIANA VALENCIA**

My family loves taking AC Transit buses. These days we use it more than BART, and it'll be how our kids get to middle school soon. But AC Transit has been struggling, and most of the elected board just serves as a rubber stamp for management instead of advocating for making the system work better for riders. Just follow my ward's director (and my friend) [Jean Walsh on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/therealjeanwalsh.bsky.social) to see how the board (other than Jean herself and Sarah Syed) is failing riders.

AC Transit recently changed from a combination of district-elected members and at-large members to only districts, so formerly at-large director Joel Young is now running in this district (Alameda, Piedmont, and part of Oakland). Young is terrible: [back in 2022]({{< relref "202211.md#ac-transit-director-at-large-alfred-twu" >}}) he had already been credibly accused of domestic violence and had been censured by the AC Transit Board for using district property for personal gain... and he still got re-elected, and this year he [physically harassed](https://oaklandside.org/2026/09/16/joel-young-ac-transit-investigation-gender-based-harassment-sarah-syed/) Director Syed (which the board majority is [trying to squelch](https://bsky.app/profile/bryanculbertson.com/post/3mxwn5z5rx224)). Oh, and in addition to all his personal failings, he also votes on the wrong side of every split vote I've looked at.

Vote for Adriana Valencia instead. She's endorsed by Walsh and Syed and will move the board in a positive direction politically and personally.


### Berkeley City Council

My district doesn't have an election this term, but four others do. There are lots of candidates and lots of issues to care about, and fortunately no serious candidate for City Council in Berkeley is ever particularly conservative, so it's reasonable to focus on more specific local policies.

When I vote at the city level, policies that keep my families safe in the streets are one of my top priorities. If you support street safety, the candidates to vote for are:

- District 1: vote for **both** Daria Wrubel and Ross Bernet as your #1 and #2 in some order
- District 4: Igor Tregub
- District 7: Syrak Micael
- District 8: Mark Humbert
